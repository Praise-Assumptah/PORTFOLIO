Why I chose this path

Software and security answer two questions I keep asking: how do things work, and how do they fail?

A system is only as good as the trust people can place in it.

Backend is where software has to be right

Databases, logic and reliability decide whether an app works for thousands of users. I enjoy that responsibility. My quiz website taught me that a feature only counts when real students can rely on it.

Mathematics makes messy things clean

Fourier series split a noisy sound into simple waves I can keep or remove. That same habit of breaking a problem into parts is how I approach code and debugging.

Security protects people, not just servers

Mobile money, school records and everyday apps all depend on someone thinking like an attacker first. Ethical hacking lets me find weaknesses with permission, before anyone harmful does.
